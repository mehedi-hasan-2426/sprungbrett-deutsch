## Content And Difficulty Contract

Every weekly addition has ten vocabulary entries with English meanings, forms,
and original bilingual examples; three recognition items, three guided completion
items, and three open reading responses. Existing exercise and flashcard IDs stay
unchanged. Repeated core terms in different weeks retain separate scheduling IDs.
Reading and writing task instructions remain German. Interface labels and hints
remain English. Open answers and writing are self-assessed, not exact-match graded.
